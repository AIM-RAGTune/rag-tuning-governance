# Limitations

- The experiment is derived from frozen sanitized CRAG mock-API observations, not a new raw-data export.
- Quality is proxy-plus-evidence, not human calibrated and not generative LLM validated.
- Frozen-observation resplits are weaker than independent split or alternate-corpus replication.
- No raw CRAG query text, raw source documents, or raw API responses are included.
- RAG Compass superiority remains unsupported.
